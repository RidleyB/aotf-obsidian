Ascendants are the immortal servants of Nimyr, Tiefling elevated beyond mortality through devotion or the secretive rituals of the Dawnspire. Silver-skinned, radiant, and demigod-like in presence, they wield the power of Nimyr’s argent light to dominate both battlefield and mind. Ascendants enforce the empire’s rule, oversee labor colonies, and act as the bridge between the mortal world and Nimyr’s domain. Their existence is eternal; should they fall, they are said to return in Nimyr’s domain.

Over time, [[Tiefling|Tieflings]] came to be, as resulting continuation of Ascendants through the centuries of Mordt's history. 

**Ascendants are currently not playable in the Ashes of the Fallen campaign.**

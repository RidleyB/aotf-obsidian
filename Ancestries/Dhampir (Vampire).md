Those of the vampiric lineage are born or bitten of blood touched by the Hungering of Thagar, manifesting traits both human and predatory. Their hunger is tempered, their strength formidable yet mortal, they can walk in Nimyr's Light with care, though the dark calls to them always. 

Vampiric-born characters possess keen senses, unnatural reflexes, and an aura that unnerves both beasts and men, but must contend with a subtle, ever-present thirst and the societal stigma of their kind. They are the children of the red wolf, balancing survival, morality, and the seductive whisper of the night.

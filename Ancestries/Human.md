Humans are the resilient inheritors of the lost world, scattered survivors of a homeland swallowed by time and shadow. On Mordt, they dwell freely mostly in the outer provinces though indentured workers, slaves, and other downtrodden groups of Humans exist closer to the Dawnspire. They muddle on, living under Nimyr’s light and the ever-present threat of Ascendants. Fierce, adaptable, and driven by survival, humans can be idealists, opportunists, or hardened pragmatists. Many carry the memory of their vanished world even centuries after, shaping their lives with a stubborn hope that the darkness can one day be pushed back.

The old human king, King Vael, led the old kingdom to join Nimyr, forming the first Ascendants.

Prior to arriving on Mordt, Humans had fallen from grace, having lost in a cataclysmic event in Human history known as the [[Shattering]]. This event led to a mass dissociative fugue, where much of Human history was forgotten or lost. Those still clinging to whatever semblance of faith they remembered could see through the lies of Nimyr in the early days.

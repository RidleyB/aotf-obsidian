Imar is a [[Celestial]] whose colossal remains are part of the world of Mordt. [[The Beastman's Curse]] is the final blight left by Imar upon the mortal world.

---
# DM

Imar's connection to the curse should remain hidden from players during the early campaign.

The existence of Imar's blight gives the eventual story of Aedar a deeper purpose. Ending Nimyr's influence alone may not heal the world, because some of Mordt's darkness originates in an older, more fundamental wound.

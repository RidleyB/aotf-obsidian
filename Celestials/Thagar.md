Thagar is an ancient, dark [[Celestial]] responsible for the [[Vampires|vampire's curse]]. The curse predates both Nimyr and the arrival of humanity on Mordt. Thagar is not currently awakened, but a great power associated with him is stirring. His resurgence has coincided with the return of vampirism over the last several centuries.

---
# DM

One possible later campaign path involves Thagar approaching the party, particularly if a character becomes a vampire. He may offer to take control of the Dawnspire, ending Nimyr's dominion while preserving its light.

This would solve one problem while introducing another: whether replacing Nimyr with Thagar would merely exchange one form of domination for another.

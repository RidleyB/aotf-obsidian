A colossal citadel of white metal and solid light, rising like a blade into the heavens. It is said to pierce the veil between the mortal realm and [[Nimyr's Domain|Nimyr’s radiant domain]]. From its highest platform, the Eternal Argent Dawn projects light across the entire world. Few other than Nimyr's closest Ascendants ever see the inner halls, where it’s rumored that living angels of silver flame stand guard.

The Dawnspire is nested atop the northeastern mountain peaks of Mordt. Its light provides warmth and illumination to the surrounding continent. It's great height ensures the range across nearly the entire land; though the farther one travels from it, the less hospitable the land becomes.

---
# DM

The Dawnspire is the ultimate strategic objective of the campaign. Its destruction would have two major consequences:

1. The light and warmth sustaining much of Mordt would falter.
    
2. The connection between Mordt and Nimyr's domain would be weakened or severed, potentially banishing Nimyr from the mortal world.
    
This creates an important tension: destroying the Dawnspire may liberate Mordt from Nimyr while also depriving its people of the light on which they depend.
